Spindlee Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Summary: Spindlee has no advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or developer-operated server. The developer does not receive your music, listening history, identifiers, or motion data.

Information used by the app

  • Apple Music library data: album, playlist, artist, song, playback, and artwork information made available after you grant Media & Apple Music permission.
  • Local music files: compatible audio files and artwork you place in the Spindlee Music folder through Files.
  • Motion data: device orientation used transiently to animate reflections. It is not recorded or transmitted.
  • Preferences and favorites: appearance, playback, sorting, and locally selected favorite items.

Apple services

Apple Music browsing, playback, metadata, and artwork may communicate with Apple through MusicKit and Apple-hosted media services. Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy and your Apple Account settings. Spindlee does not route it through a developer-operated server.

Storage, sharing, and deletion

Preferences, favorites, library indexes, and artwork caches are stored on your device. Local audio remains in Files. The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. You can revoke Media & Apple Music and Motion & Fitness permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its on-device data.
